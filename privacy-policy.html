This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS game Shatter Kingdoms (the “Game”), published by Umit Turk (“we”, “us”), handles information. In short: we do not collect any personal data ourselves. The Game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may process limited data as described below, unless you purchase “Remove Ads + Unlimited Lives”.

1. Information we collect

We do not operate accounts, servers or analytics for the Game. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, best times, lives) and your settings (sound, haptics, graphics, troop-send choice) are stored only on your device, for example in iOS app storage and device backups. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the Game or by deleting the app.

2. In-app purchases

The optional purchase “Remove Ads + Unlimited Lives” is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, Apple ID or billing address. The Game only asks Apple whether the purchase is owned on this Apple ID, so it can remove ads and enable unlimited lives, including when you tap Restore Purchases.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not purchased “Remove Ads + Unlimited Lives”, the Game shows optional rewarded videos (for an extra life or double starting troops) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. These ads are served by Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To deliver, measure and limit fraud on ads, Google may collect and process:

If you buy “Remove Ads + Unlimited Lives”, the Game does not start the advertising SDK at all, so no advertising data is processed.

4. Your choices: consent and tracking

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): before any ads load, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform asks for your choices. You can change them anytime in Settings → Premium → Privacy Choices.
  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS asks whether the Game may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and you see non-personalized or contextual ads. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Declining consent or tracking never limits gameplay. Every level can be completed without ads or purchases.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete; if you believe a child has interacted with ads in the Game, you can disable tracking in iOS settings or contact us.

6. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you reset progress or delete the app. Data processed by Google is retained under Google’s policies. We do not transfer any data to third parties ourselves.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or KVKK in Türkiye), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Since we do not hold personal data about you, most requests concern advertising data held by Google, which you can manage through your consent choices above and at myadcenter.google.com. We are happy to help: contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If the Game’s data practices change, for example a new feature, we will update this page and its effective date.
