Frequently asked questions

How do I send troops?

Drag from one of your blue castles to any castle and release. Choose how many troops to send (25%, 50%, 75% or 100%) with the SEND buttons at the bottom. Pass your finger over more of your castles on the way to send from all of them at once.

How do lives work?

You have up to 3 lives. Starting a level uses one; winning gives it back. A new life arrives every 30 minutes, and you can watch an optional video for an extra life.

What does “Remove Ads + Unlimited Lives” include?

A one-time purchase that removes every ad and lets you play without ever spending lives. The double-starting-troops bonus also becomes free.

I bought it but still see ads, or I changed devices.

Open Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the Game: Settings → Premium → Privacy Choices (shown where a consent form applies). For tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

A level feels too hard.

Gather troops in one castle by reinforcing it from others, specialize castles (Barracks train faster, Fortresses defend better), use bridges to open flanks, and attack the weakest castle first. Every level can be won without ads or purchases.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved on your device only. It is removed if the app is deleted or Reset progress is used, and cannot be recovered by us.
